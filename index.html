How it works

From “bonjour” to ordering dinner

  1. Pick where you're going

    Choose the language and your travel date. TripSpeak plans how much to learn each day.

  2. Practise a few minutes a day

    Listen, read, write and speak, with pronunciation feedback. Smart review brings back the phrases you forget.

  3. Use it on the trip

    Open the offline phrasebook and show a big card to someone, or point the camera at a menu.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which languages can I learn?

French, Italian, Spanish, Catalan, English, German, Turkish, Greek, Japanese, Thai, Arabic and Mandarin Chinese. Each course keeps its own progress, and your streak and daily goal count them all.

Is TripSpeak free?

The first lesson of every topic is free. Plus is a monthly or yearly subscription, and the yearly plan starts with a one-week free trial for new subscribers.

Does it work offline?

Yes, lessons and the phrasebook work without a connection. The camera translator may download a language once, which needs internet.

Why does an AI feature say “Not available on this device”?

The AI features run on the phone itself and need a compatible iPhone with Apple Intelligence turned on, or a supported Android phone with Gemini Nano.

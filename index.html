Speak the language of your trip.

Privacy Policy Support

TripSpeak teaches the phrases you will actually use on your trip, in French, Italian, Spanish, Catalan, English, German, Turkish, Greek, Japanese, Thai, Arabic and Mandarin Chinese: ordering food, asking for directions, checking in. No grammar tables, just ready-made phrases and short exercises, with a plan that adapts to your travel date. There is no account to create and no server in the middle.

  • Listen, read, write and speak, with pronunciation feedback.
  • Smart review brings back the phrases you forget.
  • An offline phrasebook with big cards to show to someone.
  • Plus: private AI conversation practice, a camera translator for menus and signs, AI practice sentences and explanations of your mistakes. All of it runs on your phone. The AI features need a supported iPhone or Android phone.
  • For iPhone and Android, taught in many languages.
  • TripSpeak was called TravelFrench. Your French progress carries over.

Questions? See Support. To learn what TripSpeak does with your data (almost nothing), read the Privacy Policy.
